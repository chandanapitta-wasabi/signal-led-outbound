# Follow-up templates

Fictional skeletons. Every proof point you add must be a real result you can document. Never fill these with invented numbers.

## Touch 1: first email (all tiers)

> Subject: [short, specific, no tricks]
>
> [Personalisation line from Claude, one sentence.]
>
> [One sentence connecting the signal to a relevant business problem, only where supported by the available evidence.]
>
> [One proof point.]
>
> [One ask, for example: "Worth a short call?"]

## Touch 3 (Tier 1) or touch 2 (Tier 2): a new angle

Rule: say something the first email did not. Do not summarise it and do not write "just checking in".

> Hi [Name],
>
> One more thought on [problem]. [How a similar company handled it, in two sentences, from a case you can document.]
>
> If [smaller ask, for example: "a 10-minute walkthrough of how that would look for you"] is useful, say so and I will send times.

## Last touch: permission-based close

> Hi [Name],
>
> I will stop here so I do not fill your inbox. If [problem] becomes a priority later, reply with a word and I will pick it back up.
>
> Either way, good luck with [something specific from their signal, for example "the hire"].

## LinkedIn touches (Tier 1 only)

Connection request, no pitch:

> Hi [Name], I follow [their space] and wanted to connect.

One short question, sent after they accept:

> [One neutral question that assumes nothing about how they work today, for example: "What prompted the search for a Head of Marketing at this stage?"]
