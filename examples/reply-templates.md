# Reply templates

Fictional examples. Claude drafts from these, and a person approves every reply to a positive or a referral. Replace the bracketed parts with real details from the lead's record.

## Positive

> Hi [Name],
>
> Glad this landed at the right time. Pick a slot here: [booking link]. If none of those suit you, tell me what does and I will send an invite.
>
> I will keep it to 20 minutes and bring [one specific idea tied to their signal].

## Not now

> Thanks, [Name]. That is useful to know. I will check back on [date they gave, or "in a couple of months"]. If anything changes before then, reply here.

## Referral

To the original contact:

> Thanks, [Name]. I appreciate the pointer. I will reach out to [Referral name] and mention that you suggested it.

To the person they named:

> Hi [Referral name],
>
> [Name] at [Company] suggested I contact you about [problem]. [One line on the signal, for example: "You are hiring a Head of Marketing while pipeline still sits with the founders."] [One proof point that can be documented].
>
> Open to a short call?

## Not interested

> Understood, [Name]. Thanks for letting me know. I will not follow up again.

## Unsubscribe confirmation

This is the only reply that sends without approval.

> Done, [Name]. You will not hear from me again. Sorry for the interruption.

## Unclear

> Hi [Name],
>
> To clarify: I help [ICP] with [problem]. I wrote because [signal]. Is [role] the right person for this, or should I contact someone else?
